# DivaCrew: Product

Tagline: "Your girls. Your voice. Your crew."

DivaCrew is a women-only social app where girls can hang out, organize, stay informed, and look out for each other.

Positioning: Social. Safe. Informed.

Audience: women and girls in India (college students, teens 16+, young professionals). Treat the audience as potentially including minors.

Tone: warm, playful, Gen-Z friendly, never condescending. Use phrases like "Girl, same 💅" instead of "Like". Keep UI copy short.

Core pillars: Social (feed, Fit Check), Community (Crews, event groups, chat), Civic (What Changed, Explain This, later), Safety (check-ins, reporting).

Rules for every feature:
- Women-only, privacy-first, minimal data collection.
- Never claim a place or person is "safe". Show community reports with dates, never a verdict.
- SOS and check-ins are tools, not a replacement for emergency services (112 in India). Say this in the UI.
- Civic content must be neutral: summarize, link primary sources, separate fact from opinion. Never persuade.
- Fashion features must never comment on bodies or push beauty standards.
